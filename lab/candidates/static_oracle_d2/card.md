# static_oracle_d2

- **Idea:** a diagnostic, not a method. On arena `d2` (demand ×2), every CAV drives one fixed route for the whole run: its table-best route in a (CAV, route) table pooled from the training days (301–900) of the five finished `d2` runs with 600 training days (`ref_tabular_iql`, `tabular_q_decay`, `annealed_revision`, `hedge_group`, `aon_fixed`; `ref_aon` left out because its days 501–600 are test days). Test days 901–1000 are not used to choose routes. Built by `python lab/diag/make_oracle.py --arena d2 --max-day 900 --training-eps 600 --out static_oracle_d2`.
- **Changes relative to the reference:** no learning, no exploration; 600 nominal training days so that the test days are 901–1000 like every other `d2` run. 828 CAVs; routes 0/1/2/3 used by 423/242/81/82; 72.7% coincide with the reference's final routes.
- **Why:** at ×1 the own-route table predicts `t_CAV` near the arrangements methods reach but fails for a distant one (`static_distant`). At ×2 the load effect per extra vehicle is about ten times larger. This run asks whether, under heavy load, the table still predicts an arrangement that is moderately far from the reference's (27% of CAVs moved), and how much room there is above the reference.
- **Prediction (written before the run):** the routes were chosen on days 301–900. Valued on the test days 901–1000 of the same five runs, a window disjoint from the selection (810 of 828 CAVs have such a cell with at least 3 logged days; the other 18 are left out of the forecast): **5.572**. The reference on the same days: 5.694, so the prediction is −2.1%. The training-day table gives 5.772 in-sample and 5.779 split-half, but training days at ×2 are slower for every run (the reference's CAVs: 6.35 in training, 5.69 at test), so that number is not the forecast. Tolerance ±1.0% (window-to-window spread of an unchanged policy at ×2 is up to 5% across windows, but this run tests on the same days as the table's test cells; the day-paired SE of a mean difference at ×2 is about 0.02–0.05 min, 0.4–0.9%). Band 5.516–5.628.
  - Inside the band: the own-route table still predicts a moderately distant arrangement at ×2.
  - Above the band: moving 27% of the fleet at ×2 changes the times it was chosen on (interaction, R4 direction).
  - Below the reference by more than 1%: there is room above every learner at ×2 that none of them found.
- **Drop if:** not applicable as a method.
