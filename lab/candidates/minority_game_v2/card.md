# minority_game_v2

- **Idea:** a real minority game per origin–destination pair. Each CAV holds four fixed random strategies (tables from the pair's recent history to a route) plus one "always shortest". Every training day each strategy earns +1 if the route it would have picked carried fewer of the pair's CAVs than the average eligible route that day (the less crowded, minority side), −1 otherwise. The CAV plays its best-scoring strategy. The history is the pair's least-used route on the last two days.
- **Changes relative to `minority_game`:** v1 scored strategies by the estimated travel time of the recommended route and kept the fastest route as history, so it had no reward for the less crowded option (independent review). v2 uses the minority payoff. Strategies only draw routes within 30% of the shortest free-flow time (median pair: three of four routes) (`max_detour`), so that "less crowded" is not won by driving a long detour. Pairs with fewer than three CAVs have no crowd to avoid and keep v1's time-based scores (`min_pair`). Test: the route played most often in the last 50 training days, as in v1.
- **Why it may help:** El Farol / minority-game result: heterogeneous history-based strategies spread a population over scarce options without communication.
- **Prediction:** worse than the reference: between −1.5% and −0.3% on seed 40. F4 and F10 say crowding within a pair costs little here, so a payoff for avoiding the crowd mostly pushes CAVs off their individually best route. A result at or above the reference would mean spreading matters more than F4 says. Toy model: 7.07 against v1's 4.84 (numbers not comparable to SUMO, direction as predicted).
- **Drop if:** `t_CAV` is not better than the reference on the screen seed by the arena threshold.
